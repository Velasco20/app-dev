## Favorite Series: *86 (Eighty-Six)*

**86 (Eighty-Six)** is a sci-fi war drama anime set in the Republic of San Magnolia, a nation claiming to fight an ethical, "unmanned" war against the autonomous drones of the Giadian Empire. In reality, the mechs are piloted by persecuted minorities from the hidden 86th District, who are treated as subhuman expendables on the front lines.

### Main Characters
* **Shinei "Shin" Nouzen (Undertaker)** – The stoic captain of the Spearhead Squadron who carries the burden of his fallen comrades.
* **Vladilena "Lena" Milizé** – A young, idealistic Major assigned as the Spearhead Squadron's Handler who fights against her nation's prejudice.
* **Raiden Shuga (Wehrwolf)** – The reliable vice-captain of the Spearhead Squadron and Shin's closest friend.

### Why I Recommend It
1. **Emotional Storytelling:** Tackles heavy themes like discrimination, survivor's guilt, and human resilience.
2. **Incredible Direction & Cinematography:** Visual transitions and framing carry deep symbolic meaning throughout every episode.
3. **Unforgettable Soundtrack:** Composed by Hiroyuki Sawano and Kohta Yamamoto, amplifying every battle and emotional scene.

> "Even if we're forgotten, even if no one remembers us... we were here. We lived." — *Shinei Nouzen*
